# Timeline 1924–1939 for the newspaper (fact-checked 2026-09-26)

Albanian dates verified against Wikipedia EN/SQ, Bank of Albania, Hansard. World dates are well-known; each has a
Wikipedia link. **[?]** = uncertain date/detail, **[LEGEND]** = popular claim, not proven (show as „říká se, že…").
Groups: ARMY, PEAS, BEYS (landowners), REB (rebels/exiles), POL (secret police / gendarmerie), YUG, ITA, GB.

## Prologue

- **1924-12 | march.** Zog returns from Yugoslavia with Yugoslav money, his Mati clansmen and a detachment of White
  Russian officers; Fan Noli's government flees; Zog enters Tirana on 24 Dec. → opening mini-game.

## C. Recurring Albanian news hooks (fictional but plausible)

1. Blood feud (gjakmarrja) between two mountain clans — send gendarmes / pay blood money / ignore.
2. Bandits on the Elbasan road rob the post van.
3. Olive harvest in Vlorë — good or bad.
4. Tobacco from Shkodër and Elbasan — prices follow the world market.
5. A ship at Durrës — Italian goods or a smuggling scandal.
6. Italian road builders open a road or bridge.
7. Exiles in Bari and Vienna print pamphlets; an agent reports a plot.
8. A bey drives out his tenants; peasants march to the prefecture.
9. Oil in the Devoli valley — Italian AIPA vs British Anglo-Persian.
10. The king's cigarettes and rumours of poisoners (joke items).
